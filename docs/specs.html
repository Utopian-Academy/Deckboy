Specifications

What goes in, what comes out, and what it runs on.

As of Deckboy 0.99.379. For which format to choose, see the FAQ.

Video and codecs

Video files.mp4, .mov, .mkv, .avi, .webm, .m4v, .mpg, .mpeg, .wmv, .flv, .ogv, .ts, .m2ts, .mxf
Hardware decodeDirect3D 11 (D3D11VA) on Windows, VideoToolbox on macOS, VA-API on Linux — for whichever codecs the machine's GPU decodes, typically H.264 and HEVC, and VP9 or AV1 on newer GPUs. On Windows and macOS the decoded picture stays on the GPU.
10-bitHEVC Main 10, VP9 Profile 2 and 10-bit AV1 stay 10-bit on the Windows GPU path
HAPHAP in .mov is read straight to the GPU with no decoder, and every frame is a keyframe
Everything elseDecoded on the processor by FFmpeg, which comes with every download — ProRes, DNxHR and the rest play, at a higher CPU cost
Stills.png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .bmp, .gif, .tif, .tiff, .avif, .heic, .heif
SlidesPDF, one cue per page, rendered once at import by the operating system's own PDF engine; PowerPoint (.pptx, .ppt, .pps, .ppsx), Keynote (.key) and OpenDocument (.odp) decks the same way, converted by PowerPoint, Keynote or LibreOffice on the machine
Live sourcesCameras, application windows and screens, web pages, NDI, SRT, RTMP, RTSP, UDP, Blackmagic DeckLink capture, Spout (Windows)
GeneratedTest patterns, a video synth, live-coded pictures, timers, lower thirds and tone
Effects39 video effects on the live picture, every parameter drivable by an oscillator; 13 cue-to-cue transitions

Output

DestinationsDisplays (windowed or fullscreen), NDI, SRT, RTMP, RTSP and UDP streams, Blackmagic DeckLink SDI where the Blackmagic driver is installed, Spout (Windows), SMPTE ST 2110
Formats720p at 50, 59.94 and 60; 1080i at 50, 59.94 and 60; 1080p and 2160p at 23.98, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94 and 60
Bit depth8-bit or 10-bit
SMPTE ST 2110ST 2110-20 uncompressed video and ST 2110-30 (AES67) audio, with AMWA NMOS IS-04 registration and IS-05 connection management
Decks and outputsUp to 16 playlists, each output showing whichever one you choose, or several composited as layers; a multiview you arrange yourself
MappingPer-output warp and edge blending; a corner pin per layer
Operator screensPresenter view and a mirrored prompter, each an output of its own

Recording

WhatThe programme, as it plays, while it goes to every other output
CodecsH.264 and HEVC in .mp4; Apple ProRes Proxy, LT, 422, HQ and 4444 and Avid DNxHR LB, SQ, HQ and HQX in .mov
FormatAny size and frame rate, or the programme's own
TimecodeStamped from a set start or time of day, drop or non-drop frame
SegmentsSplit into files by length or by size

Audio

Sample rate48 kHz
Audio files.mp3, .wav, .flac, .aac, .ogg, .oga, .m4a, .opus, .wma, .aiff
Output channels2, 4, 6, 8, 16, 32 or 64 per deck device — enough for a Dante Virtual Soundcard
RoutingA stereo pair per cue, or a per-cue crosspoint matrix to any channel, stepped off, full, −3, −6 and −12 dB
Processing22 audio effects per cue, and your own VST3 effects and instruments in the same chain
InputA microphone or line input, by device

Show control and timecode

Remote protocolPlain-text commands over TCP or UDP on port 5510; every command answers OK or ERR with the reason
ControllersA Bitfocus Companion module and an Elgato Stream Deck plugin
MIDINotes, control changes and program changes in and out, MIDI Show Control, and MIDI files played out as cues
NetworkOSC in, with OSC Query discovery, and out; Art-Net DMX in (a lighting desk fires cues) and out (DMX cues); Blackmagic ATEM switcher tally and triggers
TimecodeAn LTC generator and LTC chase in the macOS and Linux downloads
Running orderStandby and GO, pre-wait, post-wait and continue, arm and disarm, audition, preload, and a pre-show CHECK
Show-control cuesTarget, fade, master, MIDI, network (OSC, UDP, TCP), timecode, DMX and script cues

System

Windows64-bit, installer or portable zip; also through Scoop
macOSmacOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel; also through Homebrew
Linuxx86-64: an AppImage for Ubuntu 22.04 and anything newer, a portable tarball, or a Flatpak bundle
Show filesOne file opens on all three
Interface37 languages
LicenceGPL-3.0, free. No account, no licence server, and nothing needs the internet to play a show
