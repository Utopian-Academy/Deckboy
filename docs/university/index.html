Video University

This is a free course in video for people who run shows. It starts at "what is a pixel?". It continues to networks, lenses, tape, HDR and new codecs. Each lecture has a simulator that you can use in this page. You do not need an account: your progress stays in your browser.

PROF. VIDEO

I teach the history, the signal and the science. I also get lost in patches.

DR. PIXEL

I teach what you plug in, what breaks, and what you check before doors, skapi.
