Switches, cables and Wi-Fi

DR. PIXEL

A switch connects the devices. A router connects networks. A simple show often needs only a switch.

Use wired connections for anything that matters. Use your own switch. The venue's Wi-Fi is shared with everybody in the room, and you do not control it. When the audience arrives, the Wi-Fi gets slower. That is the worst moment.

Try it: how full is the cable?

The rates are examples. Check the real rate of your own format and device.

DR. PIXEL

Keep some room. A link that runs near 100% drops packets, and then video breaks up. I plan to stay under about 70%, skapi. That is my own rule, and it has served me well.

Read the speed on the switch. One old 100 Mb/s switch in the chain limits everything behind it. It is often the one nobody remembers.
