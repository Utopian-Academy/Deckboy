Colour from three lights

PROF. VIDEO

Those three numbers are red, green and blue. A screen makes each pixel from three small lights. Mix the three lights and you get the colour.

It is additive, like three oscillators into one mixer. More signal, more light. All three at full is white. All three at zero is black.

Try it: mix three lights

The colour on the right is .

PROF. VIDEO

Left: the three lights, seen close. Right: the colour your eye sees from a distance. Your eye mixes them.

Try red and green with no blue. You get yellow. That surprises people who learned to mix paint.

DR. PIXEL

Each number here goes from 0 to 255. That is 256 steps for each light, or 8 bits. Three lights of 256 steps give 16,777,216 colours.

Professional video often uses 10 bits, which is 1,024 steps for each light. More steps make smoother gradients. You will see "8-bit" and "10-bit" on spec sheets, skapi. Now you know what they mean.
