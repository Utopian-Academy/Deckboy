Resolution and shape

DR. PIXEL

Resolution is how many pixels a picture has: the number across and the number down. You will see these every day, skapi. Learn them.

Try it: the formats, to scale

DR. PIXEL

Double the width and double the height, and you have four times the pixels. So 4K has four times the pixels of 1080p. More pixels need more data, more cable speed and more computer.

Careful with the word "4K". Television 4K, or UHD, is 3840 x 2160. Cinema 4K is 4096 wide. They are different. Read the numbers.

PROF. VIDEO

The p in 1080p means progressive: each frame has all of its lines. You will also see 1080i, which means interlaced. That is a later course, and a good one.

DR. PIXEL

Now shape, which we call aspect ratio: width divided by height. Most video today is 16:9. A 16:9 picture on a screen of a different shape cannot fill it perfectly. You must choose.

Try it: a 16:9 picture on another screen

DR. PIXEL

Black bars are not a fault. They show that the shapes do not match. Ask about the screen shape before the show, skapi, and not at the show.
