What the mask means

DR. PIXEL

The subnet mask says which part of the address is the street, and which part is the house. Devices on the same street can talk directly.

You see it two ways, and they mean the same thing: 255.255.255.0 and /24. The 24 means that the first 24 bits are the street.

Try it: are they neighbours?

DR. PIXEL

Try /25 in the simulator. 192.168.1.20 and 192.168.1.200 stop being neighbours, although they look like neighbours. So always read the mask, skapi. And both devices need to use the same mask.

PROF. VIDEO

Why the odd numbers? Each number is 8 bits. 255 means all 8 bits belong to the street. It is a binary gate on every bit. A /24 leaves 8 bits for houses: 256 values, but the first and last are reserved, so you get 254 devices.

DR. PIXEL

For a show, use /24 unless somebody tells you different. 254 devices is enough for most shows, and everybody can read it.
