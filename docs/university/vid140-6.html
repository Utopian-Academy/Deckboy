Finding each other

DR. PIXEL

How does the laptop know that the camera exists? Some devices announce themselves. NDI sources commonly use mDNS for this.

That announcement does not normally cross a router. So when a source does not appear in the list, the video may be fine. Only the announcement is missing.

Try it: why is it not in the list?

DR. PIXEL

Now draw your network on one page. Do it before the show. A good page has:

A list of every device, with its name, address and location.
The mask.
Which switch each device goes to, and which port.
What runs on the network: NDI, Art-Net, OSC, Companion.
Who to call.

Tape it to the rack, skapi. Tomorrow-you deserves kindness.
