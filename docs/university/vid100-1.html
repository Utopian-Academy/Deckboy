What is video?

PROF. VIDEO

Welcome. We start with the first question. What is video?

Video is many still pictures, shown one after the other. That is all. There is no movement in any one picture. Your brain makes the movement.

It is like a sequencer. Each step is still, but play the steps quickly and you hear a pattern. Each still picture is one frame.

Start at 1 frame per second. You see a ball that jumps from place to place. Now move the slider up slowly. At some point, the jumps become movement.

Your screen may not refresh at more than 60 times each second. Thus, this simulator stops at 60.

DR. PIXEL

Skapi, you will hear these numbers every day: 24, 25, 30, 50 and 60. That is frames per second, or fps. Film uses 24. Countries with 50 Hz power mostly use 25 and 50. Countries with 60 Hz power mostly use 30 and 60.

Know the rate of your clip. Know the rate of your screen. When they do not match, some equipment must repeat or drop frames.

PROF. VIDEO

One warning. People say that the eye has a frame rate. It does not. Researchers measure different results for different people, different brightness and different parts of the eye. There is no single number.
