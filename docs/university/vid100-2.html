What is a pixel?

DR. PIXEL

Dobar den, skapi. A frame is a picture. Now look closer. A digital picture is a grid of small squares. Each square has one colour. Each square is one pixel.

The word is short for picture element.

Try it: change the number of pixels

Move your pointer over the picture to read one pixel.

DR. PIXEL

Move the slider to the left. You can count the squares. Move it to the right. The squares become too small to see, and you see a picture.

Now point at one square. It has three numbers. A pixel is only numbers, skapi. The next lecture explains them.

PROF. VIDEO

Here is some history. In 1957, Russell Kirsch made one of the first digital images at the US National Bureau of Standards. It was a scan of a photo of his baby son, only 5 centimetres square.

The word "pixel" came later. It was in print by 1965. I like that the idea is older than the name. Like most of my patches.
