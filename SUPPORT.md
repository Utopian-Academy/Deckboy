# Help & community

Deckboy is free, open-source software for live video playback and show control. You're welcome here whether you're a programmer, a first-time operator, or somebody trying to get a stubborn signal to the screen.

## Where to go

## Make a useful bug report

Please include the Deckboy version, OS, CPU/GPU, signal path (for example, camera → Deckboy → NDI → receiver), expected result, actual result, and steps to reproduce. Logs and screenshots help.

**Protect the show.** Before attaching logs, screenshots, or a show file, remove client names, credentials, private IP addresses, sensitive URLs, stream keys, and licensed or confidential media. Never post passwords or security details in a public issue.

## Before doors

Deckboy is community-maintained software, not a staffed emergency support service. GitHub issues and discussions are not monitored in real time, and no response time is guaranteed. Test with your actual hardware and signal chain before relying on a build during a live show. Keep your known-good fallback ready.

## Be excellent to each other

Ask questions generously, answer them clearly, and respect operators of all experience levels. Constructive criticism and bug reports are welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, and sharing other people's private show information are not.
