# Lesson media

## mosh_reel.mp4

The practice clip for the datamosh lesson: twelve shots and eleven hard cuts,
each a place where datamosh drags one picture into the next. Every cut lands on
a bar of the music (84 BPM). 640x480, 24 fps. Every clip is cropped to 4:3,
scaled and colour-boosted; shot 10 is also cropped at the top to remove a
caption bar. No clip is shown unaltered.

The NASA footage is public domain. NASA's media guidelines apply: it is not
copyrighted, no endorsement is implied, and the NASA insignia is not used as a
logo; markings on vehicles are incidental. NASA pages are
https://images.nasa.gov/details/<ID>.

## The music

The sound is the Deckboy theme (the STAIRLIFT version), written for Deckboy and
played by Deckboy's own synth.

## around_the_mix.mp3

The Sound lesson's song, "Around the Mix" by the robot DJs: an original house groove at
121 BPM that travels through gamelan bells, an Afrobeat guitar cell, a Balkan reed, Andean
flutes, a Punjabi tumbi and dhol and a Colombian cumbia, while the robots chant "around the
mix". Made for Deckboy with Deckboy's own synth (`--synth-render`) and its Experimental voice.
