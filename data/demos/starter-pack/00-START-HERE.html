DECKBOY / EXAMPLE SHOWS

Output first.
Then take your first cue.

Start from the extracted folder

On Windows, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Open the folder it creates. Keep the media folder beside the shows.

Deckboy saves your changes automatically. Use these extracted files; opening an individual show inside a ZIP gives Windows a temporary copy.

In Deckboy 0.99.417 or later, choose OPEN and select 01-start-here.deckboy. Choose SAVE AS (Ctrl + Shift + S) to make your own copy in the same folder.

STEP 1 / ACTIVATE THE OUTPUT

Press N

This opens the Programme output window. You can also open DASH (Ctrl + D) and click 1 Activate output.

The output starts black because you have not taken a cue yet. Press F to switch fullscreen/windowed. On one screen, use your window switcher to return to the Deckboy desk.

For an external display, select it under Settings → Video Outputs.

STEP 2 / SEND A CUE

Select cue 01, then press Enter

TAKE sends the selected cue to your output. You can use the TAKE button on the desk or 2 Take selection in DASH.

Your output now says YOUR OUTPUT IS LIVE.

STEP 3 / ADVANCE THE SHOW

Press Space

GO takes the next cue: a Swirl made live by Deckboy. Keep going to explore Video Synth, an effect and the closing card.

To jump to a specific cue, select it and press TAKE. To close the output, press N again or choose Output off in DASH.

Three more shows to explore

  • 02 — Layers and titles: choose the named scenes in DASH.
  • 03 — Effects playground: compare six looks and change their controls.
  • 04 — One-button show: activate output, then press Run sequence in DASH for a 39-second show with original music.

Each cue includes notes. The full guide is in README.md.
