WHY THEY ARE BUNDLED
--------------------
Deckboy looked different on every platform. Nothing was bundled but the pixel
face, so the sans and mono fell through to whatever the machine had: Segoe UI
on Windows, Arial or Helvetica on macOS, Liberation Sans on Linux. Same build,
three different-looking desks — and the splash was the most obvious place it
showed.

Liberation Sans is the right one to carry rather than any other, because the
layout is already built around its metrics: it is metric-compatible with Arial
and close enough to Segoe UI that the fixed-width chips and button labels fit.
That is exactly why the Linux font search already preferred it over DejaVu Sans,
which is markedly wider and ellipsized labels that fitted everywhere else.

A machine's own fonts remain in the search as fallbacks, so a build without this
directory still runs.
