# Deckboy community code of conduct

**Be kind, be curious, and make room for the next operator.**

Deckboy is built for people who do demanding work in real venues, from first-day learners to engineers who have spent years behind a switcher. We want our forums, reviews, chats and meetups to feel welcoming without making technical criticism taboo.

## Our expectations

- Respect different backgrounds, levels of experience, and ways of communicating.
- Criticize ideas, designs, bugs and tradeoffs without insulting people.
- Help newcomers ask better questions instead of dismissing them.
- Share technical details without posting client-private materials, show files, access tokens, stream keys or credentials.
- Use relevant channels and keep unsolicited promotion and spam out of the community.
- Respect others' boundaries; harassment, stalking, threats, discriminatory abuse and sexual harassment are not welcome.
- If you find a security vulnerability, [report it privately](SECURITY.md) instead of publishing exploit details.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and, where possible, warn or restrict people who repeatedly break these expectations. Serious harassment and disclosure of others' private information may result in immediate removal or reports to the hosting service. For misconduct on GitHub, use the platform's report and block tools. Don't post personal allegations publicly in an attempt to get moderator attention.

Moderation of any future Discord server will use that service's reporting and permissions tools. We will link an appropriate private community-moderation contact once one is operating.

## Good-faith engineering discussion

It's fine to disagree strongly about live-show practices, reliability, test procedures, UI design, hardware and playback workflows. Specific evidence and reproduction steps make good arguments; insults don't. A cute mascot and an operator-grade signal chain can belong to the same program.

This document covers Deckboy project spaces and does not replace the policies of the platforms hosting them.
